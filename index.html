Object to the gambling planning application at 403 Holloway Road

A change of use to a 24-hour Adult Gaming Centre (slot-machine gambling) has been submitted for 403 Holloway Road, on the corner of Parkhurst Road (application P2026/1808/FUL). Local people can object on planning grounds.

Comments should reach the council by 5 September 2026

Why residents are objecting

  • 1Over-concentration: two other 24-hour Adult Gaming Centres are already within view of the site. Another breaches the Local Plan's cumulative-impact test (paragraph 4.72).
  • 2High deprivation: this area is more deprived than 83.6% of England and Wales, and 98% when it comes to older people, a key consideration under Local Plan paragraph 4.131.
  • 3A flawed risk assessment: the applicant's report badly understates how close schools, nurseries, pawnbrokers and support services really are.

Take action

This form drafts a planning objection for you, freshly worded every time so that each submission counts on its own rather than as one repeated petition. Add your details, change anything you like, then send it to the planning team (planning@islington.gov.uk). The reference P2026/1808/FUL is included for you.

You must include your name and address or the objection will not be counted. They are shared with the applicant but removed from the documents published online.

A sentence in your own words, why this matters to you or your family, carries real weight. It is added near the end of the email.
